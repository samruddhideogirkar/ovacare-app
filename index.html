About PCOD/PCOS

PCOD/PCOS is a hormonal disorder affecting 1 in 10 women of reproductive age.

Common signs: irregular cycles, acne, excess hair growth, weight gain.

Causes include genetics, insulin resistance, and hormonal imbalance.

Diagnosis: blood tests, ultrasound, and menstrual history.

Management: healthy diet, regular exercise, medications to regulate hormones.

Early detection reduces risks like infertility, diabetes, and heart disease.
